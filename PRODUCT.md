# Product

## Register

product

## Users

AI Study Assistant is for students, researchers, and technical learners who keep dense PDFs, notes, and Markdown files locally and want a dependable way to ask cited questions, compare sources, review weak points, and turn documents into practice material. They may also be showing the project in an interview, so the first-run path needs to communicate credibility quickly.

## Product Purpose

The product turns local study materials into a searchable, cited, quiz-ready knowledge base. Success means a user can add material, ask a grounded question, understand why the answer is trustworthy, and continue into review or exploration without learning the app's internals first.

## Brand Personality

Calm, technical, and trustworthy. The interface should feel like a focused knowledge tool rather than a generic AI demo: quiet where the user is reading, explicit when evidence or system state matters, and confident without decorative noise.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS landing-page chrome, rainbow AI gradients, oversized hero metrics, chat apps that hide provenance, and dashboard pages that look impressive but do not help the next action. The product should not feel like a disconnected collection of feature demos.

## Design Principles

1. Evidence is a first-class object: answers, citations, debug traces, and source previews should be easy to understand without leaving the workflow.
2. The next action should always be visible: upload, ask, compare, review, or inspect should never require guessing where to go.
3. Familiar beats flashy: use standard product patterns for navigation, lists, forms, filters, and destructive actions.
4. Local-first should feel reassuring: connection, storage, indexing, and failure states need plain language and recoverable actions.
5. Interview mode should show the system's depth quickly while still feeling useful for daily work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast for core text and controls. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences, readable placeholders, and mobile layouts without horizontal overflow. Do not rely on color alone for important status.
